## 1. The Shortlist (8 Sites)

### Category A: 5 Sites in Industry, Infrastructure, Climate Tech & B2B Operations

#### 1. Epic Cleantec (`https://epiccleantec.com`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Onsite commercial wastewater treatment & water circularity for high-rise commercial real estate.
* **Why it is great:** It fundamentally redefined wastewater branding. It strips away the "dirty sewer" stigma and presents water recycling as an elite architectural feature for premier developers. Uses a clean light palette with sky-blue and emerald accents, crisp typography, and high-impact photography of real mechanical piping and treatment modules.
* **Signature effect:** Staggered horizontal solution modules with smooth image-scale reveals and floating circularity badges.

#### 2. Form Energy (`https://formenergy.com`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Multi-day energy storage and deep grid infrastructure.
* **Why it is great:** Exemplary balance of industrial engineering weight and Silicon Valley executive craft. Generous whitespace, deep executive slate typography, and clinical hairline borders.
* **Signature effect:** Split-screen asymmetric hero with mechanical hardware close-ups and bold monospace engineering telemetry metrics (`100 Hours`, `10 MW`).

#### 3. Watershed (`https://watershed.com`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Enterprise climate platform and ESG audit accounting.
* **Why it is great:** The industry gold standard for displaying complex compliance data, audit logs, and environmental metrics. Crisp white cards, precise tabular borders, and zero clutter.
* **Signature effect:** Interactive audit report card with smooth micro-tab transitions, live filter calculations, and compliance certification stamps.

#### 4. Cambrian Innovation (`https://cambrianinnovation.com`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Industrial wastewater treatment & "Water Energy as a Service" (WEaaS).
* **Why it is great:** Direct B2B positioning for commercial and industrial facilities. Treats wastewater tanks and bioreactors with architectural photography and bold operational SLAs.
* **Signature effect:** Interactive operational timeline with live yield counters ($\ge 95\%$) and scheduled maintenance markers.

#### 5. Ketos (`https://ketos.co`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Industrial water quality intelligence, automated lab testing, and EPA/OSHA compliance monitoring.
* **Why it is great:** High-trust telemetry aesthetic. Displays chemical parameter matrices (pH, BOD, COD, TSS, heavy metals) with clinical tabular clarity that mirrors a certified laboratory testing console.
* **Signature effect:** Real-time sensor parameter table with compliant/non-compliant visual indicators and automated audit status badges.

### Category B: 3 Sites from Unrelated Industries with Exceptional Motion Craft

#### 6. Linear (`https://linear.app`)
* **Industry / Niche:** High-performance software development systems.
* **Why it is great:** Unmatched micro-interaction polish. Fluid 60fps responsiveness, keyboard accessibility, subtle hairline borders (`border-black/[0.08]` / `border-white/[0.08]`), and tactile click feedback.
* **Signature effect:** Cursor-following spotlight border glow on cards, fluid tab switching with shared layout animations (`layoutId`), and zero-latency page responsiveness.

#### 7. Stripe Press (`https://press.stripe.com`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Ideas and technology publishing.
* **Why it is great:** Benchmark for editorial typography scale, tactile lighting, subtle grain and layered elevation, and asymmetric grid-breaking layout rhythm.
* **Signature effect:** Inertia-based smooth scrolling, subtle 3D tilt interaction on physical cards, and masked headline text reveals on scroll.

#### 8. Polestar (`https://polestar.com`)
* **Industry / Niche:** Minimalist electric performance vehicles.
* **Why it is great:** Supreme Scandinavian light-theme minimalism. Razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, massive viewport-filling imagery with tight padding, and cinematic pacing.
* **Signature effect:** Scrubbed video/image pin scroll storytelling where physical engineering components disassemble and highlight as the user scrolls.

## 2. Our Primary & Secondary Picks

### ★ PRIMARY REFERENCE: Epic Cleantec (`https://epiccleantec.com`)
* **Tone & Audience Fit:** Exactly identical audience: Facility Directors, Commercial Real Estate Developers, and Property Asset Managers.
* **Why it wins:** It proves that commercial wastewater infrastructure can be presented with the aesthetic of high-end Swiss architectural design. It uses a **pristine light palette with light blue/teal precision accents**, high-contrast executive navy typography, and clean horizontal modules.
* **Technical Feasibility:** 100% achievable in Next.js using Lenis for smooth scroll, GSAP/ScrollTrigger for pinned card reveals, and Framer Motion for interactive verification tables and modals.

### ★ SECONDARY REFERENCE 1: Watershed (`https://watershed.com`)
* **Role:** **Audit & Data Architecture**.
* **What we borrow in principle:** The executive audit card layout. The way Watershed displays compliance metrics, verification ledgers, and tabular proof points will directly inform our **Monthly STP Performance Report Card** (Water Balance, Lab Test Parameters, Equipment Availability, WhatsApp Evidence).

### ★ SECONDARY REFERENCE 2: Linear (`https://linear.app`)
* **Role:** **Micro-Interaction & Hairline Polish**.
* **What we borrow in principle:** The micro-interaction polish. Subtle spotlight borders, refined hairline dividers (`border-sky-200/80`), tactile button clicks, and zero-latency tab transitions.
